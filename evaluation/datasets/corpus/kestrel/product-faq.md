# KR-7 Robot Product FAQ

The KR-7 is Kestrel's autonomous warehouse robot.

## Specifications

The KR-7 carries a maximum payload of 12 kg and has a top speed of 1.8 metres per second. It is rated IP54, which means it is protected against dust and splashing water but must not be used outdoors in rain.

## Battery and Charging

A full battery lasts about 9 hours of normal operation. Charging from empty to full takes 2.5 hours on the standard dock. Robots return to the dock automatically when the battery falls below 15%.

## Warranty

The KR-7 comes with a 24-month warranty covering manufacturing defects. Damage caused by misuse, water immersion, or unauthorised repairs is not covered.

## Firmware

Firmware updates are released monthly and installed automatically overnight while the robot is docked. Customers can pause automatic updates for up to 30 days from the dashboard.
