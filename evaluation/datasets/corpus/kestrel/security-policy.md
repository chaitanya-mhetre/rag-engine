# Information Security Policy

Security is every employee's responsibility. Violations of this policy may lead to disciplinary action.

## Passwords and Authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across systems. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts. Administrators must use a hardware security key (YubiKey) as their second factor; SMS codes are not allowed for administrators.

## Devices

All company laptops must use full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may access email only through the managed mobile app. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 1 hour.

## Incident Reporting

Report suspected security incidents to security@kestrel.example within 1 hour of discovery, or call the security hotline on extension 4471. Suspected phishing emails should be forwarded to phish@kestrel.example and then deleted.

## Data Classification

Kestrel classifies data into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted. Restricted data, such as customer payment details and employee health records, may only be stored in approved encrypted systems and must never be shared over chat.

## Access Reviews

Managers review their team's system access every quarter. Access for departing employees is revoked on their last working day.
