# Expense Policy

Kestrel Robotics reimburses reasonable business expenses that are submitted with the required documentation.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance is INR 1,500 for domestic travel and USD 60 for international travel. Team meals require prior approval from a department head. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Receipts and Submission

Itemised receipts are required for every expense above INR 500. Expenses must be submitted in the Ledgerly expense tool within 30 days of being incurred; claims older than 90 days are rejected automatically.

## Approval Limits

A direct manager can approve individual claims up to INR 50,000. Claims above INR 50,000 require approval from the Finance Director. Claims above INR 2,00,000 also require CFO sign-off.

## Mileage

Use of a personal car for business travel is reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre. Two-wheelers are reimbursed at INR 6 per kilometre. Tolls and parking are reimbursed at actual cost with receipts.

## Non-reimbursable Items

The following are not reimbursed: alcohol, traffic fines, personal entertainment, lost baggage fees, and upgrades purchased without approval.
