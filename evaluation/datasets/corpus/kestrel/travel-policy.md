# Travel Policy

All business travel must be booked through the SkyDesk travel portal. Bookings made outside SkyDesk are not reimbursed unless SkyDesk was unavailable.

## Flights

Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires approval from the CEO. Flights should be booked at least 14 days before departure whenever possible.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is INR 8,000 in metro cities and INR 5,000 in other cities. For international travel the nightly limit is USD 200. Employees staying with friends or family instead of a hotel may claim a flat INR 1,000 per night.

## Travel Insurance

All employees are covered by the corporate travel insurance policy while travelling on business. The policy number is KR-TRV-2291. In a medical emergency abroad, call the insurer's 24-hour assistance line printed on the back of the travel card.

## Visas

The company pays for business visas. Visa applications should start at least 6 weeks before travel. The People team provides invitation and support letters.
