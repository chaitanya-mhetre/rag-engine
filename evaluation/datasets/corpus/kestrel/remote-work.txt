Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Kestrel Robotics follows a hybrid model. Employees are expected in the office three days a week. Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are core office days.

New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of INR 25,000 to buy a desk, chair, or monitor. Internet costs are reimbursed at INR 1,000 per month.

Employees may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year, with prior approval from their manager and the People team. Working from abroad for longer requires a tax review.

Fully remote arrangements are approved only by the department head and are reviewed every six months.
