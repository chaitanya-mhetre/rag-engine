Employee Benefits

Health Insurance

Group health insurance covers the employee, their spouse or partner, and up to two children, with a sum insured of INR 5 lakh per year. Parents can be added to the policy with a 20% co-pay on claims.

Wellness

Every employee receives a wellness stipend of INR 2,000 per month, which can be used for gym memberships, sports, or therapy sessions.

Learning Budget

Each employee has a learning budget of INR 40,000 per year for courses, books, and conference tickets. Unused learning budget does not roll over.

Equity

Eligible employees receive stock options that vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. After the cliff, options vest monthly.
