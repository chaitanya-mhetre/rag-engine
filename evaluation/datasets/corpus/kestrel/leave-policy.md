# Leave Policy

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Kestrel Robotics. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis.

## Vacation

Every full-time employee receives 22 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Vacation accrues monthly, at a rate of 1.83 days per month. Up to 5 unused vacation days may be carried over into the next calendar year; any additional unused days expire on 31 December. Vacation requests must be submitted in the Tern HR system at least 10 business days in advance for absences longer than three days.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry over. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave

The primary caregiver receives 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which may be taken at any time within the first 12 months after birth or adoption. Parental leave requests should be raised with the People team at least 8 weeks before the expected start date.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member, and up to 2 days for extended family.

## Public Holidays

The company observes 10 public holidays each year. The holiday calendar for each office is published in Tern every December.
