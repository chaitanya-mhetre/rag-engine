# New Joiner Onboarding

Welcome to Kestrel Robotics. This guide explains what happens during your first weeks.

## Day One

On your first day you collect your laptop from the IT desk at 10:00 and meet your onboarding buddy. Your buddy is an experienced colleague from a different team who answers informal questions during your first 90 days.

## Mandatory Training

All new joiners must complete security awareness training within their first 7 days. Engineers must also complete the production access course before they are granted access to the fleet cloud.

## 30-60-90 Plan

Your manager agrees a 30-60-90 day plan with you during your first week. It lists the goals you are expected to reach at 30, 60 and 90 days.

## Probation

The probation period is 6 months. During probation the notice period is 2 weeks; after confirmation it becomes 2 months.
